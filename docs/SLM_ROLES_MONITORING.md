# Prashanth Hospitals — Service Line Manager (SLM) Governance Guide

## 1. Role Definition
Service Line Managers (SLMs) are Department Patient Managers responsible for managing patient leads from initial contact through doctor consultation, surgery fixing, and post-visit recovery across all Prashanth Hospitals branches.

## 2. SLA Benchmark KPIs
* **First Response TAT**: < 15 Minutes
* **Contact Rate**: > 85%
* **Follow-up Compliance**: > 92%
* **Appointment Conversion**: > 60%

## 3. SLA Escalation Thresholds
* **15 Mins**: Mobile App Push Warning to SLM
* **30 Mins**: Alert Banner on Branch Head Dashboard
* **60 Mins**: Auto-reassign lead to backup SLM in department
